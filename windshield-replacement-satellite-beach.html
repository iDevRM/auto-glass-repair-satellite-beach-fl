What Does Windshield Replacement Involve?

Windshield replacement starts with covers over the hood, fenders and dash. The technician cuts the old glass free from its urethane bond and trims the old adhesive down to an even layer. On coastal cars the pinch weld around the opening is checked closely, because salt air can start rust under an old seal, and any bare metal is primed. Fresh urethane goes down, the new windshield is set and centered, and the mirror, sensors and moldings are reinstalled. The install takes about an hour, plus cure time.

When Do You Need Windshield Replacement?

A crack longer than a dollar bill

Long cracks cannot be stopped with resin and keep running.

Damage in the driver’s view

Resin leaves a faint mark, so breaks right in front of the driver need new glass.

A crack at the edge

Edge cracks weaken the bond between glass and frame and spread fast in heat.

Heavy pitting and glare

Glass worn hazy by sand and salt makes sunrise and night driving hard.

A leak at the top corners

Water inside after a rainstorm often points to a failed or rusted seal.

Glass broken by storm debris

Flying fronds and loose items in tropical weather crack or cave in windshields.

Why Do Windshield Cracks Spread So Fast at the Beach?

A windshield is two layers of glass with a clear plastic layer in between, and a stone chip breaks the outer layer. Each time the glass heats up and cools down, it expands and shrinks, and that pulls at the ends of the break. Cars parked in full sun near the ocean get very hot, then the AC hits the inside with cold air. Humid, salty air also gets into an open chip and weakens the edges of the break.

Frequently Asked Questions

How soon can I drive after a new windshield in Satellite Beach?

Most urethane adhesives reach safe drive-away strength in one to three hours, depending on the product, the heat and the humidity. The technician gives you the exact time before leaving. Driving too soon can let the glass shift or weaken the bond the airbag relies on.

What if there is rust under my old windshield?

Light surface rust on the frame is cleaned and primed before the new glass goes in. Deep rust that has eaten through the metal needs body shop repair first, because the adhesive cannot bond to it. We tell you before any work starts if we find it.

When can I wash my car after a windshield replacement?

Wait at least a day before a car wash and keep pressure washers away from the edges of the glass for a couple of days. A gentle fresh-water rinse to remove salt is fine after the first day.

Do I need ADAS calibration with a new windshield?

If there is a camera behind the rearview mirror, the answer is usually yes. The camera has to be aimed again for the new glass so lane-keep and emergency braking work as designed. We confirm it for your vehicle with the estimate.
