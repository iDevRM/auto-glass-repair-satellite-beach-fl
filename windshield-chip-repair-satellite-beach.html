What Does Windshield Chip Repair Involve?

Chip repair starts with cleaning loose glass, sand and salt residue out of the impact point and drying any moisture trapped inside. A bridge tool attaches over the chip, pulls a vacuum to draw out air and then pushes resin into every leg of the break. UV light cures the resin, and the surface is scraped flat and polished. A short single crack gets a tiny hole drilled at its end so it stops running, then it is filled the same way.

When Do You Need Windshield Chip Repair?

A fresh star or bullseye

The sooner resin goes in, the cleaner the repair looks and the better it holds.

A chip smaller than a quarter

Most chips up to about this size are good candidates for repair.

A short crack from a chip

Single cracks under about six inches can often be stopped and filled.

Two or three small chips

Several small chips can usually be repaired in the same visit.

After a causeway trip

Gravel trucks on the causeways throw a lot of small stones.

Before summer heat

Hot glass and cold AC are when chips spread fastest.

Why Do Rock Chips Happen So Often Around Satellite Beach?

Rock chips come from small stones thrown up by the tires of the vehicle ahead. Drivers on the island cross the Pineda and Eau Gallie causeways to reach the mainland and I-95, and construction and landscaping trucks on A1A and South Patrick Drive carry gravel and shell. Sand blowing off the dunes also leaves small pits that can become chips. A stone hitting glass at highway speed is enough to break the outer layer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will a repaired chip disappear completely?

A resin repair makes a chip much harder to see, but a faint mark often remains where the stone hit. The main goal is strength: the resin bonds the broken glass so it cannot spread. Chips repaired soon after they happen look the best.

Can I drive right after a chip repair?

Yes. The resin is cured with UV light before the technician leaves, so the car can be driven and washed right away. There is no adhesive cure time like there is after a full windshield replacement.

How do I keep a chip from spreading until it is fixed?

Put a piece of clear tape over it to keep out sand and moisture, park in the shade and avoid pointing the defroster or AC straight at the glass. Slammed doors and hard bumps can also spread it, so book the repair soon.

Can sand pitting be repaired like a chip?

No. Resin works on a single break, not on hundreds of tiny surface pits. Glass that is hazy and pitted all over usually needs a new windshield if the glare bothers you.
