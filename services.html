Windshield Replacement

Windshield replacement removes the whole front glass and bonds a new one to the frame. It fixes the safety problem a long crack creates: weakened glass that may not hold the roof or the passenger airbag the way it should in a crash.

Drivers need it when a crack is longer than a dollar bill, reaches the edge of the glass or sits in the driver’s view. Left alone, a crack keeps growing with every bump and every blast of cold AC on hot glass.

Call us today for windshield replacement in Satellite Beach. Read more about windshield replacement.

Windshield Chip Repair

Windshield chip repair fills a small break with clear resin, cures it with UV light and seals the surface. It solves the most common glass problem on the island: a fresh chip from a stone thrown on A1A or a causeway.

Anyone with a chip smaller than a quarter or a short single crack should call soon. Humid salt air and dirt work into an open chip within days, and heat turns many chips into cracks that need a new windshield.

Call us today for windshield chip repair in Satellite Beach. Read more about windshield chip repair.

Pitted Windshield Replacement

Pitted windshield replacement swaps out glass that has been sandblasted, hazed and scratched by years of coastal driving. It solves a problem resin cannot fix: thousands of tiny pits that scatter light from the sun and from oncoming headlights.

Drivers who park near the dunes, drive A1A every day or commute east into the sunrise notice it first. Ignoring it means squinting through glare, wipers that chatter and a windshield that is harder to see through every year.

Call us today for pitted windshield replacement in Satellite Beach. Read more about pitted windshield replacement.

Back Glass & Door Windows

Back glass and door window replacement installs new rear glass, liftgate glass or side windows after a break-in, a stray object or a storm. It solves a car that cannot be locked or kept dry.

Car owners need it right away, because an open window lets in rain, heat, salt air and anyone walking by. Broken tempered glass also scatters into the cargo area, door panels and seat tracks, so the cleanup matters too.

Call us today for back glass & door windows in Satellite Beach. Read more about back glass & door windows.

ADAS Camera Calibration

ADAS calibration re-aims the forward camera behind the windshield so lane-keep assist, automatic emergency braking and adaptive cruise read the road correctly. It solves the hidden problem new glass can create: a camera aimed a tiny bit off.

Owners of most vehicles built in recent years need it after a windshield replacement. Skip it and the safety systems may warn late, nudge you toward the lane line or shut off with a dash light.

Call us today for ADAS camera calibration in Satellite Beach. Read more about ADAS camera calibration.
