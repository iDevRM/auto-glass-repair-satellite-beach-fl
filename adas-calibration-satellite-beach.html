What Does ADAS Camera Calibration Involve?

ADAS calibration starts with a scan of the vehicle’s computer to see which driver-assist systems are installed and whether any fault codes are stored. A static calibration places a printed target at a measured distance in front of the parked vehicle while the camera learns it. A dynamic calibration has the vehicle driven on clearly marked roads at a steady speed while the camera relearns the lane lines. Some vehicles need one method and some need both.

When Do You Need ADAS Camera Calibration?

After a windshield replacement

The camera is mounted to the glass, so new glass changes its angle.

A dash warning after glass work

Lane or collision lights after an install mean the camera needs attention.

Lane-keep pulling the wrong way

A camera aimed slightly off can steer you toward the lane line.

After an alignment or suspension work

Ride height and wheel angle changes affect what the camera sees.

After a minor front-end bump

A small collision can shift the aim of cameras and sensors.

Warnings that come late

Collision alerts that arrive too late are a sign the camera is off.

Why Does a New Windshield Affect the Camera?

The forward camera sits in a bracket glued to the inside of the windshield, just behind the mirror. Every windshield sits a tiny bit differently in its frame, and a change of a fraction of a degree moves where the camera is looking far down the road. The vehicle’s computer cannot correct that on its own, so the camera has to be calibrated to the new glass.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my car has ADAS?

Look for a camera housing behind the rearview mirror at the top of the windshield, or check whether your vehicle has lane departure warning, lane-keep assist, automatic emergency braking or adaptive cruise control. Any of these usually means a camera that needs calibration after new glass.

Is calibration required after a windshield replacement?

Most vehicle makers say yes. Their repair procedures call for calibration whenever a windshield with a camera bracket is replaced. Skipping it can leave the camera aimed slightly wrong, which makes warnings late or steers the lane-keep the wrong way.

How long does ADAS calibration take?

Calibration usually takes 30 minutes to an hour, depending on the vehicle and the method. Some vehicles finish a dynamic calibration in a short drive, while others need a longer run on clearly marked roads.

Will I get proof the calibration was done?

Yes. You get a record showing the calibration was completed, which helps with your insurance claim. Keep it with your vehicle paperwork.
